# Current world canon

`docs/WORLDBUILDING.md` is authoritative. This file is a routing summary, not a
second lore bible.

## World identity

- Tone: light, adventurous high fantasy with consequential but bounded danger.
- Art: high-definition oil-brush painterly 2D anime in layered 2.5D scenes.
- Grounding: region-specific history, climate, materials, labor, trade, dress,
  architecture, and institutions.
- Magic: authored rules with costs, limits, tells, and counterplay.
- People: culture and experience drive behavior; fantasy ancestry is not a moral
  alignment or monoculture.

## First region

Whitemarch Basin is the first production region. Whitemarch is a fortified
river-and-road capital tied to nearby farms, market towns, workshops, ferries,
shrines, and frontier routes. Its useful institutions and competing charters
create choices without making cruelty its defining spectacle.

Complete this region's communities, professions, factions, encounter ecology,
and campaign arc before expanding the world map.

Legacy region, faction, race, and item files may contain names worth reviewing,
but they do not override the current product and worldbuilding authorities.
