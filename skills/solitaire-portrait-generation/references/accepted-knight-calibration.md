# Accepted Knight calibration

Inspect `../assets/calibration/knight-observed-moment-approved-preview.png` when a
request needs the approved Solitaire portrait grammar.

This file is a **composition and art-direction calibration only**. It is an opaque
RGB image with a baked checkerboard and must never be copied into runtime assets.

## What the user approved

- A handsome adult Knight observed while tightening a gauntlet.
- Attention directed off-camera, serious relaxed mouth, uneven shoulders, and a
  natural torso turn rather than a roster pose.
- One dominant weathered vermilion cloak family with quiet iron, charcoal, leather,
  skin, and subdued brass.
- Close head-to-mid-thigh scale with a large readable face.
- A broad full-height cloak mass on the outer right side, governed by wind and
  gravity rather than a narrow shoulder streamer.
- A sword carried high at the screen-left waist, tucked behind the bent armored
  forearm. The pommel sits inward/up-right, the guard down-left near the belt, and
  the scabbard continues down-left through the lower editorial crop.
- Intentional continuation of long cloth, clothing, and scabbard beyond the crop;
  no artificial zoom-out to display their ends.

## What must not propagate

Do not reuse the Knight's face, gender, age, hair, gesture, armor, vermilion color,
or sword placement by default. Each archetype needs its own observed moment,
silhouette motif, equipment logic, and single primary pigment family.
