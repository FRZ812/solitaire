# Modular archetype portrait prompt template

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opaque chroma-key source for a half-body dark-fantasy mobile RPG combat portrait

Input images:
- Images 1-5 are mandatory style and medium references only.
- Learn their hand-painted watercolor/gouache anime finish, expressive face design,
  airy colored midtones, simplified readable costume masses, selective ornament,
  and visible brush character.
- Do not copy any reference character, costume, pose, anatomy, prop, magic object,
  architecture, or background.

Primary request: Create a new generalized [ARCHETYPE] portrait with a distinct,
memorable identity that remains modular rather than depicting a named lore character.

Subject: [ARCHETYPE-SPECIFIC AGE, GENDER PRESENTATION, FACE, HAIR, EXPRESSION,
COSTUME, EQUIPMENT, AND ONE CLEAR SILHOUETTE MOTIF]. Depict an observed moment:
[OFF-CAMERA ATTENTION OR PRACTICAL INTERRUPTED GESTURE]. The character is living
through the instant rather than posing, presenting, or smiling for the viewer.
For a magical archetype, use practical scholarly or working garments and let the
observed behavior, book/tool, and one restrained magical tell carry the class read;
avoid ornamental RPG robes, armor-like filigree, ceremonial collars, repeated arcane
embroidery, and gemstone coverage.

Style/medium: premium painterly anime illustration in traditional watercolor and
gouache character; soft pigment transitions, lightly textured brush edges, elegant
facial planes, expressive eyes, selective crisp accents. Clearly hand-painted and
illustrative, never glossy 3D, photographic, or hyper-rendered digital concept art.

Composition/framing: portrait orientation; one character; head-to-mid-thigh
three-quarter stance; face unobscured and dominant; compact readable silhouette;
natural asymmetry in gaze, shoulders, hands, and weight; props close to the body.
Leave visibly empty key matte above the complete hair/headwear silhouette and two
uninterrupted key-matte columns along the full left and right edges. Nothing—hair,
clothing, hands, weapons, or props—may touch or exit the top or either side. Only
lower clothing may crop through the horizontal bottom edge. Face height 17-20%
of canvas, eye line 18-23% from top. Do not widen, squeeze, or otherwise distort
the body to fill a standardized box.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly uniform opaque [PURE CHROMA GREEN #00FF00 OR PURE CHROMA
MAGENTA #FF00FF] selected opposite the costume palette. The matte fills every
non-character pixel and every gap between hair, fingers, straps, and equipment.
No transparency, checkerboard, texture, gradient, environment, floor, cast shadow,
halo, vignette, or panel. The matte is unlit and must not spill onto the character.

Lighting/mood: soft illustrated key light with colored ambient fill; readable face
and upper torso; quiet confidence appropriate to [ARCHETYPE].

Color palette: one bright archetype-defining pigment family, [PRIMARY HUE FAMILY],
with tonal variation inside that hue. Keep supporting cloth, armor, leather, and
hardware neutral or low-chroma so they never compete with the primary. No equal
complementary-color split. Preserve airy midtones and painted variation.

Constraints: opaque uniform chroma matte; clean firm antialiased edges; one character;
coherent hands and equipment; large costume shapes before detail; generalized
late-medieval fantasy construction; no text or watermark. Final asset will be
normalized to 960x1280 RGBA with source aspect ratio preserved and checked at
120x145. Preserve the accepted close subject scale and body proportions during
edits. Every visible prop must have realistic construction,
support, and attachment and never intersect anatomy, clothing, armor, or another
prop. Long scabbards and lower garments may intentionally continue beyond the bottom
crop when their direction and perspective make that continuation unambiguous; never
zoom out just to reveal their tips. Do not let anything exit a side or the top. Character
pigment must meet the chroma matte directly. Do not add paper-white crop paint, pale
erosion, a dry-brush cutoff, rim light, glow, feathering, or light-colored underpainting.

Avoid: literal copying from references, photoreal skin, glossy 3D rendering,
over-sharpening, excessive etched micro-detail, crushed blacks, harsh white rim,
paper-white crop fade, pale eroded hem, white dry-brush cutoff, white matte or fringe,
fake transparency, nonuniform key matte, keyed spill, same-face beauty, chibi anatomy,
competing bright complementary colors, generic two-tone game-skin palettes,
sexualized costume unless explicitly required, giant props, full-body framing,
direct eye contact, presentation smile, centered character-select pose, clipped
heads/hands/joints, top/side edge contact, accidentally truncated equipment, floating equipment,
impossible grips, fused anatomy, unrequested zoom-out, scenery, particles, UI,
symbols, frame, text, watermark.
```
